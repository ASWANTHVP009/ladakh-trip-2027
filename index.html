Distance summary: approximately 3,645 km going + 685 km Ladakh local circuit + 3,260 km return = about 7,590 km total. These are indicative planning estimates, not verified navigation totals. Actual mileage will change with detours, accommodation locations and road closures.
Important feasibility note: All requested places, the Kerala–Ladakh round trip, at least 48 hours of acclimatisation in Leh, Nubra/Turtuk and Pangong cannot be comfortably completed in 22 days with safe daily riding limits. The framework below exposes the long days rather than disguising them. I strongly recommend adding 3–5 days or dropping stops. Never skip acclimatisation, ride exhausted, or continue through unsafe weather to meet the schedule.

Day-by-day itinerary framework

Approximate kilometres only; actual distances depend on road selection, diversions and accommodation. Verify roads, permits and weather shortly before travel.

Day 1 · Balussery → Mangaluru

~270 km
6 AM · 6–8 hrs
  • Kozhikode – Kannur – Kasaragod – Mangaluru.
  • Breakfast around Kannur; fuel and lunch breaks.
  • Stay: private hotel with secure parking.

Day 2 · Mangaluru → Lonavala

~650 km
6 AM · very long day
  • Ride north via the selected Goa/Konkan–Pune corridor.
  • Frequent breaks; no sightseeing detours.
  • Stay: Lonavala hotel.

Day 3 · Lonavala → Udaipur

~650–700 km
6 AM · very long day
  • Use a suitable Maharashtra/Gujarat highway route.
  • Split this leg if fatigued; do not ride late to make up time.
  • Stay: Udaipur.

Day 4 · Udaipur → Ajmer → Jaipur

~400 km
6 AM
  • Udaipur: Lake Pichola / City Palace if time allows.
  • Ajmer visit; allow time for parking and walking.
  • Continue to Jaipur; stay in Jaipur.

Day 5 · Jaipur → Amritsar

~650–700 km
6 AM · long day
  • Jaipur: Hawa Mahal / Amber Fort only if visit time is protected.
  • Delhi bypass and Punjab highway corridor.
  • Stay: Amritsar.

Day 6 · Amritsar local

~40–70 km
Early start
  • Golden Temple (Sri Harmandir Sahib).
  • Jallianwala Bagh if time permits.
  • Attari–Wagah ceremony; verify visitor timings and restrictions.
  • Stay: Amritsar.

Day 7 · Amritsar → Pathankot

~120 km
6 AM
  • Short transfer; inspect tyres, brakes, chain and luggage.
  • Stay: Pathankot.

Day 8 · Pathankot → Srinagar

~350–400 km
6 AM · allow extra time
  • Via Jammu and the highway/tunnel corridor.
  • Check traffic, security and weather advisories.
  • Stay: Srinagar.

Day 9 · Srinagar → Sonamarg → Kargil

~200–220 km
6 AM · mountain road
  • Sonamarg stop; proceed only if Zoji La is open.
  • Cross Zoji La in daylight and follow traffic control.
  • Kargil War Memorial at Dras if open and time allows.
  • Stay: Kargil.

Day 10 · Kargil → Lamayuru → Leh

~220 km
6 AM · mountain road
  • Lamayuru Monastery / Moonland viewpoint.
  • Reach Leh; rest and avoid exertion.
  • Stay: Leh.

Day 11 · Leh acclimatisation

0–10 km
No high-pass ride
  • Rest, hydrate, eat light and monitor both riders.
  • No Khardung La, Chang La or strenuous sightseeing.
  • Stay: same Leh hotel.

Day 12 · Leh acclimatisation

0–15 km
Complete at least 48 hours before higher excursions
  • Continue resting; seek medical help for concerning symptoms.
  • Gentle local movement only if both feel well.
  • Stay: Leh.

Day 13 · Leh local / buffer

20–40 km
Only if symptom-free
  • Leh Palace, Shanti Stupa, Hall of Fame or market.
  • Use as weather, road or health contingency if needed.
  • Stay: Leh.

Day 14 · Leh → Nubra → Turtuk

~200–220 km
6 AM · high altitude
  • Cross Khardung La only if open and both riders are well.
  • Diskit and Hunder if time allows.
  • Continue to Turtuk; verify local entry requirements.
  • Stay: Turtuk / Nubra registered homestay.

Day 15 · Turtuk → Pangong Tso

~200–250 km
6 AM · road-condition dependent
  • Shyok route via Agham / Shyok / Durbuk only if officially open and safe.
  • Water crossings and damaged surfaces can make it unsuitable.
  • Stay: registered Pangong camp or guesthouse.

Day 16 · Pangong → Leh

~220 km
6 AM
  • Early lake visit; return via Chang La only if open.
  • Stay: Leh; refuel and prepare for the Manali highway.

Day 17 · Leh → Jispa

~330–350 km
6 AM · extremely demanding
  • Via Upshi, Tanglang La, More Plains, Sarchu and Baralacha La.
  • Attempt only with favourable weather, road status, fuel and rider condition.
  • Stop earlier if conditions are poor; add a day.

Day 18 · Jispa → Rohtang → Manali

~150–190 km
6 AM · permit dependent
  • Keylong and Rohtang Pass approach.
  • Rohtang access may require permits or be restricted.
  • If closed, Atal Tunnel is an alternative but bypasses Rohtang.
  • Stay: Manali.

Day 19 · Manali → Chandigarh

~300 km
6 AM
  • Via Mandi and Bilaspur.
  • Stay: Chandigarh; rest and service check.

Day 20 · Chandigarh → Indore

~850–900 km
6 AM · not recommended in one day
  • Exceptionally long transfer; split this leg if fatigued.
  • Do not attempt a 900 km day with pillion and luggage to meet the calendar.
  • Staying en route means exceeding 22 days.

Day 21 · Indore → Hyderabad

~700 km
6 AM · long transfer
  • Major highway route; schedule fuel, food and rest stops.
  • Stay: Hyderabad.

Day 22 · Hyderabad → Balussery

~850–900 km
6 AM · not recommended in one day
  • Return via Karnataka towards Kerala.
  • This is too long for a safe one-day couple ride.
  • Plan an overnight stop; the full journey will exceed 22 days.

Couple accommodation plan

Accommodation budget set by you: ₹22,000 total for the entire trip. If the plan uses 21 overnight stays, this averages about ₹1,050 per night for both people. This is a very tight budget for private rooms across tourist and remote Himalayan destinations; expect basic guesthouses/homestays, shared facilities in some places, and possible extra cost at Pangong, Turtuk, Leh and Manali. Treat ₹22,000 as a target cap, not a guaranteed bookable price.

Bike & document checklist

Motorcycle

  • Full service before departure
  • Tyres, brakes, chain and sprockets
  • Clutch cable, fuses and basic tools
  • Puncture kit and portable inflator
  • Check payload and luggage mounting
  • Emergency fuel only in approved, legal containers

Rider & pillion

  • Certified full-face helmets
  • Armoured jackets, gloves and boots
  • Waterproof layers and thermal wear
  • Rain suits, first-aid kit and personal medicines
  • Offline maps and power banks
  • Warm layers even in July

Documents & permits

  • DL, RC, insurance and PUC
  • Original photo ID
  • Check Ladakh fee and route permissions close to departure
  • Verify Wagah visitor access/timings
  • Check Rohtang permit rules
  • Check restrictions for border-area destinations

Final checks before July 2027

  • Monitor monsoon, landslide and flash-flood advisories.
  • Verify current Ladakh acclimatisation guidance and permits.
